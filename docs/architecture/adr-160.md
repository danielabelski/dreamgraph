# ADR-160: Slice 5 — Closed 11-kind card taxonomy with assertNeverCard exhaustiveness; outcome and fallback are first-class kinds

**Date:** 2026-05-09T21:53:20.598Z  
**Status:** accepted  
**Decided by:** collaborative  
**Affected entities:** extensions/vscode/src/architect-v2/cards/types.ts, extensions/vscode/src/architect-v2/cards/factory.ts  

## Context

v1 had ad-hoc card shapes added per feature, no exhaustiveness check, and no first-class surface for tool outcomes or fallback justifications. New card types appeared by string drift; renderers silently dropped unknown kinds.

**Constraints:**
- Closed union; no string escape hatch
- Cards immutable in place
- Every consumer switch must use assertNeverCard for exhaustiveness
- Outcome card's body must wrap ToolOutcome unmodified (Slice 4 ADR-157)
- Fallback card's body must carry both the ExecutionPlan and the FallbackJustification (Slice 4 ADR-158)

## Decision

Card kind is a closed union of exactly 11 literals: goal, plan, context, decision, edit, verification, blocker, next-step, completion, fallback, outcome. The `outcome` card wraps a Slice-4 ToolOutcome verbatim; the `fallback` card wraps a Slice-4 ExecutionPlan + FallbackJustification verbatim. Cards are immutable — updates produce successor cards linked by parentCardId. assertNeverCard() forces every consumer-side switch to be exhaustive. Alternatives rejected: 9-kind taxonomy from foundation alone (no first-class surface for ToolOutcome or FallbackJustification — defeats Slice 4 normalization); open enum / string escape hatch (silent drift, the failure mode v1 made too easy).

## Consequences

- Renderers can be exhaustive at compile time
- Slice 4 ToolOutcome and FallbackJustification surface as first-class card kinds
- New card kinds require a deliberate ADR + migration entry

**Risks accepted:**
- Adding a kind requires migration coordination
- Outcome/fallback cards may feel redundant when their parent edit/blocker card already exists — documentation must justify

## Guard Rails

> ⚠️ Do NOT add a new card kind without a superseding ADR and a schema bump

> ⚠️ Do NOT widen CardKind to `string`

> ⚠️ Do NOT mutate a card in place; emit a successor with parentCardId

> ⚠️ Do NOT skip assertNeverCard in card-handling switches

> ⚠️ Do NOT collapse outcome/fallback back into edit/blocker — they exist to preserve Slice 4 normalization
