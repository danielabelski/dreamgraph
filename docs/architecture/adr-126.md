# ADR-126: DreamGraph success pattern: Hypothesis → Restraint → Pressure → Resolution → Validation

**Date:** 2026-05-06T09:04:27.345Z  
**Status:** accepted  
**Decided by:** collaborative  
**Affected entities:** dreamgraph_src_api_routes, api_routes, cognitive_engine  

## Context

Recurring weak-connection tension can indicate a real architectural relationship before the graph has admissible proof. The system needs a reusable pattern describing when repeated resurfacing is healthy rather than pathological.

**Constraints:**
- Validated graph edges remain the threshold for proven fact.
- Recurring tension alone must not be promoted to truth without admissible structural evidence.
- Low resurfacing rate is acceptable when it preserves epistemic restraint.
- Dream-cycle linking may supply missing intermediate structure over time.

## Decision

Treat the sequence Hypothesis → Restraint → Pressure → Resolution → Validation as a positive DreamGraph success pattern when a repeatedly resurfacing tension remains unproven until later dream-cycle or graph-linking work produces a validated semantic path. In this pattern, repeated pressure is interpreted as healthy persistence of uncertainty, not failure, provided the system continues to withhold truth claims until validated edges exist.

### Alternatives Considered

- **Suppress recurring tensions earlier to reduce repeated resurfacing** — Rejected: This would reduce pressure on unresolved but meaningful hypotheses and could hide real but not-yet-provable relationships.
- **Promote recurring weak connections to fact after enough repetitions** — Rejected: This would weaken epistemic discipline and risk converting strong intuition into unsupported truth claims.

## Consequences

- DreamGraph narratives can distinguish healthy repeated hypothesis pressure from faulty churn.
- Operators have a named reference pattern for interpreting low-rate recurring tensions that later validate.
- Future design discussions can point to this pattern when evaluating dream-cycle value and tension suppression policy.

**Risks accepted:**
- Operators may overuse the pattern to justify tensions that never validate.
- A named success pattern could be misread as endorsing all resurfacing rather than only restrained, eventually validated cases.

## Guard Rails

> ⚠️ Do NOT treat recurrence count alone as proof; validated semantic graph evidence remains required.

> ⚠️ Do NOT classify resurfacing as a success pattern unless the relationship later resolves through explicit validated structure.

> ⚠️ Do NOT use this pattern to bypass investigation of genuinely noisy or pathological tension generators.
