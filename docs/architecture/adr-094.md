# ADR-094: Domain Separation

**Date:** 2026-04-29T19:00:59.452Z  
**Status:** accepted  
**Decided by:** system  
**Affected entities:** instance_management, policy_management  

## Context

To maintain clear boundaries between different functional areas of the application.

**Constraints:**
- Each domain must be isolated to prevent cross-domain dependencies

## Decision

Domain boundaries are established for cognitive processing, instance management, and policy management.

### Alternatives Considered

- **Monolithic domain structure** — Rejected: Increased complexity and potential for bugs.
- **Loose coupling without clear boundaries** — Rejected: Difficult to manage and scale.

## Consequences

- Easier maintenance and scalability of the application.

**Risks accepted:**
- Over-segmentation could lead to communication overhead.

## Guard Rails

> ⚠️ Do NOT change the domain boundaries without reviewing this ADR.
