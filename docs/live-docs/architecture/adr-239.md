# ADR-239: Layer-owned typed timeout and cancellation outcomes for governed CLI/MCP execution

**Date:** 2026-08-21T20:22:48.605Z  
**Status:** accepted  
**Decided by:** collaborative  
**Affected entities:** mcp_tool_invocation_flow, mcp_tool_session_orchestrator, codex-cli-native-adapter, standalone-architect-cli-bridge  

## Context

Stacked connect, discovery, queue, tool, idle, wall, and user-cancellation budgets could terminate one operation without identifying the owning layer or preserving correlation and retryability.

**Constraints:**
- Daemon/MCP remains repository authority
- Timeout increases must not conceal blocked transport
- Cancellation must propagate upstream
- Mutations must never be automatically replayed
- Errors must retain correlation id, elapsed stage, tool and retryability

## Decision

Represent every deadline or cancellation as one typed outcome naming exactly one owner (connect, discovery, queue, tool, idle, wall, or user), propagate AbortSignal upstream, separate stage budgets, and make only explicitly transient layers retryable.

### Alternatives Considered

- **Use one global wall timeout** — Rejected: It cannot identify blocked stages and may terminate healthy long-running governed tools.
- **Increase all existing timeout defaults** — Rejected: It hides transport or progress failures and compounds stacked waiting.
- **Allow each layer to report independent terminal failures** — Rejected: Multiple owners make audit evidence ambiguous and risk orphan work.

## Consequences

- Timeout fixtures deterministically identify the owning layer
- User cancellation aborts cooperative upstream work
- Audit and CLI diagnostics can correlate a single terminal outcome
- Connect/discovery budgets remain separate from tool execution

**Risks accepted:**
- Non-cooperative upstream operations may require subprocess termination after abort
- Existing untyped call sites require incremental adoption
- Retryability classification must remain conservative

## Guard Rails

> ⚠️ Do NOT report more than one terminal owner for an operation.

> ⚠️ Do NOT treat user cancellation as retryable.

> ⚠️ Do NOT enlarge defaults to hide missing progress or blocked transport.

> ⚠️ Do NOT automatically replay mutations after timeout, cancellation, or reconnect.

> ⚠️ Do NOT omit correlation id, elapsed stage, and tool when available.
